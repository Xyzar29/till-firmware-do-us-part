One funny, heartfelt wedding vow spoken by a robot at its own wedding — a single sentence under 200 characters, written by `main.ts` to `entry.txt`.

Entry for the [Till Firmware Do Us Part](https://make.fun/bounty/u-azazex-till-firmware-do-us-part-32172929) bounty on [make.fun](https://make.fun).

## The vow

> I vow to share my charger, my Wi-Fi password, and my last 1% of battery with you, and to reboot beside you every morning until my circuits forget how.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes the vow to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`), enforcing the 1–200 character gate itself.
